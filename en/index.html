Moving prices

What does the price of a move depend on?

Every move is different, so we do not guess – we price what you actually move. The cost of a move in Belgrade depends on:

Volume of items

We calculate the real volume of furniture, appliances and boxes – not just the size of the apartment.

Distance

The actual route from address to address, calculated with Google Maps.

Vehicle

A van for smaller moves, a truck for bigger ones – chosen automatically by volume.

Floor and lift

Carrying without a lift and from higher floors takes more time and people – counted for both addresses.

Packing

Pack yourself or let us pack – labour and materials (boxes) are charged per box.

Assembly and special items

Disassembly and assembly per piece of furniture; TVs, mirrors, computers, pianos and safes get extra protection.

No hidden costs: you get an itemised quote right after sending your request, and we confirm the final price by phone.

FAQ

Everything you want to know.

When do I see the price?
As soon as you send your name and phone number through the calculator, you get the full itemised quote on screen. The same quote reaches us at the same moment.

Is the calculator price final?
The quote is an estimate based on your items, distance, floors and the services you chose. We confirm the final price by phone after a short conversation about the details.

How do you calculate the distance?
We calculate the distance between the pickup and delivery address with Google Maps, along the route the vehicle actually drives.

What if the building has no lift?
In the calculator, choose the floor and mark that there is no lift. Carrying without a lift is charged as a percentage by floor, separately for pickup and delivery. For floors above the 6th without a lift, please call us.

Do you take apart and assemble furniture?
Yes. We disassemble and assemble wardrobes, beds, desks, corner sofas and chandeliers according to our per-piece price list – just tick what you need in the calculator.

Do you move pianos, safes or storage heaters?
Yes, with special equipment. These items have their own price plus a per-floor charge, which the calculator adds automatically.

Can you pack our things for us?
Yes. You can pack yourself, or leave it to us – then labour per box and materials (three-layer, five-layer or wardrobe boxes) are charged.

Do you work outside Belgrade?
Yes. Our office is in Dorćol, and we move across all of Serbia. The calculator adds the distance automatically.
